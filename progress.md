## 1. Executive Project Overview

### What Kundali Is and the Problem It Solves

**Kundali (कुण्डली)** is a bilingual (English / Nepali), security-hardened full-stack Vedic astrology platform built around a strict separation between **deterministic astronomical computation** and **grounded, human-reviewed astrological interpretation**. Existing consumer astrology apps either hide their astronomical assumptions (mixing ayanamsha standards, mean vs. true lunar nodes, or whole-sign vs. Bhava Chalit houses without explanation) or expose users to hallucinated AI astrology responses without verifiable source citations.

Kundali enforces a single invariant pipeline: **Compute First, Interpret Second**. Every chart quantity (Lagna, planetary longitudes, signs, Whole Sign houses, Nakshatras, Padas, retrograde flags, and boundary sensitivity alerts) is calculated deterministically via the Swiss Ephemeris C library (`swisseph-ffi`) and validated into an immutable `ChartFacts` JSON contract *before* any AI interpretation layer can run. Every AI claim must cite specific computed `fact_ids` and retrieved knowledge-base `chunk_ids` vetted by practicing astrologers, alongside explicit traditional vs. tentative confidence badges and permanent non-predictive framing.

1. **Deterministic Calculation Layer (`backend/app/calc/`):** Pure Python + Swiss Ephemeris C bindings (`swisseph-ffi`). Computes reproducible sidereal longitudes, houses, Nakshatras, and provenance (`swieph`, `moshier`, or `mixed`). Statically forbidden from importing `app.ai`, `app.api`, `app.db`, or `app.core` via [backend/.importlinter](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/.importlinter).

Every claim is cited. Every reading can be checked."*).

### 2.2 Deterministic Calculation Engine (`backend/app/calc/`)

#### Architecture Decisions & Layer Isolation
- **Swiss Ephemeris Binding ([ADR 0002](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/docs/adr/0002-swisseph-ffi-instead-of-pyswisseph.md)):** Uses `swisseph-ffi==1.0.0` (Ctypes/CDLL wrapper around Astrodienst's C library) rather than `pyswisseph`, avoiding brittle C++ build-tool compilation on Windows while retaining GNU AGPL-3.0 licensing ([LICENSE](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/LICENSE)).
- **Dependency Boundary ([ADR 0006](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/docs/adr/0006-import-linter-boundary-calc-ai.md) & [backend/.importlinter](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/.importlinter)):** `import-linter` enforces two architectural contracts: `app.calc` is strictly forbidden from importing `app.ai` AND forbidden from importing `app.api`, `app.db`, or `app.core`. [backend/pyproject.toml](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/pyproject.toml) enforces `mypy` `strict = true` on `app.calc.*`.

#### Implemented Modules & Pipeline Behavior

1. **Time Conversion & Julian Day ([timeconv.py](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/app/calc/timeconv.py)):**
   - `TimeInput` (`extra="forbid"`) accepts `date`, `time` (mandatory), finite `latitude` (`[-90, 90]`), finite `longitude` (`[-180, 180]`), and mutually exclusive IANA `timezone` or finite `utc_offset_hours` (`(-24, +24)`).
   - `convert_local_to_utc_jd()` disambiguates IANA wall clock times across `fold=0` and `fold=1`, raising distinguishable `TimeConversionError(ValueError)` with `.code` (`dst_gap`, `dst_overlap`, `invalid_timezone`, `missing_timezone_spec`, `conflicting_timezone_spec`, `invalid_utc_offset`, `invalid_coordinates`).
   - Computes UT Julian Day via synchronized `get_swe_engine()` -> `swe_julday(..., SE_GREG_CAL)` under `SWE_LOCK` and validates finite return -> `CalculationError` on internal C failure. Accurately handles Nepal Time (`UTC+5:45`, `Asia/Kathmandu`).
2. **Thread Safety & Ayanamsha ([ayanamsha.py](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/app/calc/ayanamsha.py)):**
   - Process-shared OS C library state (`swe_set_sid_mode`, `swe_set_ephe_path`) is guarded by a single reentrant lock `SWE_LOCK = threading.RLock()`.
   - `get_swe_engine()` lazily initializes `_SWE_INSTANCE` and configures `EPHE_DIR` (`backend/ephe/`, containing `sepl_18.se1`, `semo_18.se1`, `seas_18.se1`) inside `SWE_LOCK`.
   - `AyanamshaMode` supports `lahiri` (`SE_SIDM_LAHIRI`, default per [ADR 0005](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/docs/adr/0005-default-conventions-and-hamro-patro-compat-preset.md)), `lahiri_1940` (`SE_SIDM_LAHIRI_1940`), `raman` (`SE_SIDM_RAMAN`), and `krishnamurti` (`SE_SIDM_KRISHNAMURTI`).
   - `get_ayanamsha_degrees()` sets sidereal mode under `SWE_LOCK` and calls `swe_get_ayanamsa_ex_ut(jd_ut, SEFLG_SWIEPH | SEFLG_NONUT)` to preserve exact `SEFLG_NONUT` nutation convention parity with `swe_get_ayanamsa_ut` while capturing the `int` status flag and error string.
3. **Ephemeris Provenance & Planetary Positions ([ephemeris.py](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/app/calc/ephemeris.py)):**
   - Computes 7 physical grahas (`Sun` through `Saturn`) plus `Rahu` (`SE_MEAN_NODE` default or `SE_TRUE_NODE`) using `SEFLG_SWIEPH | SEFLG_SPEED | SEFLG_SIDEREAL`, deriving `Ketu = (rahu_lon + 180.0) % 360.0` with identical `speed`.
   - `Sun` and `Moon` always have `retrograde = False`; `Rahu` and `Ketu` always have `retrograde = True`; Mars–Saturn use `speed < 0`.
   - `classify_ephemeris_flag()` checks `iflag < 0` first (crucial because `-1` sets all bits in two's complement!), validates explicit auxiliary allowlists (`AYANAMSHA_AUXILIARY_FLAGS = SEFLG_NONUT`; `PLANETARY_AUXILIARY_FLAGS = SEFLG_SPEED | SEFLG_SIDEREAL | SEFLG_NONUT`), rejects unexpected return bits / `SEFLG_JPLEPH` / contradictory source bits with `CalculationError`, and aggregates body sources into `"swieph"`, `"moshier"`, or `"mixed"`.
4. **Houses, Lagna & Quantization ([houses.py](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/app/calc/houses.py)):**
   - `compute_lagna()` sets sidereal mode under `SWE_LOCK` and calls `swe_houses_ex(..., b"W"[0], SEFLG_SIDEREAL)`, verifying return status $\ge 0$ and finite `ascmc[0]`.
   - Implements Whole Sign houses (`house_of()` mapping sign distance from Lagna sign into `1..12`), `normalize_longitude()`, `quantize_longitude()` (`round(normalize(lon), 6) % 360.0`, preventing `29.9999996` -> `30.0` sign overflow), and `is_lagna_boundary_sensitive()` (`deg < 0.5 or deg >= 29.5`).
5. **Nakshatra & Pada ([nakshatra.py](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/app/calc/nakshatra.py)):**
   - Maps normalized sidereal longitudes onto the 27 Vedic Nakshatras (`Ashwini` through `Revati`, $13^\circ 20'$ each) and 4 Padas ($3^\circ 20'$ each).
6. **Immutable ChartFacts Contract ([facts.py](file:///d:/SYP/Kundali/backend/app/calc/facts.py)):**
   - `ENGINE_VERSION = "0.2.0"`.
   - Named preset `"hamro_patro_compat"` -> `{ayanamsha: "lahiri_1940", house_system: "whole_sign", node_type: "true", year_days: 365.25}` documented from South Asian reference variance analysis (constant $\approx 41''$ Lahiri vs $\approx 11''$ `lahiri_1940` difference + node convention gap).

### 2.3 Reliability & Security Milestones (Chart Calculation API Preparation)

Before exposing calculation over HTTP, a formal architecture & reliability audit identified subtle concurrency, bitwise flag, provenance, and floating-point cusp bugs in `backend/app/calc/`. These were fixed across two pushed hardening commits prior to Commit 2:

#### Commit 1 — `fix(calc): harden swisseph locking, error handling, provenance, and rounding`
- **Commit SHA (Git-confirmed):** `fae9971d64abeefb50652111cb2bd10899cee916` (`2026-11-26`, `12` files, `+1208 / -229`)
- **Bugs Fixed & Hardening:**
  1. **Bitwise `-1` masking bug:** In Swiss Ephemeris C, error flag `ERR == -1` (all bits `1`), so `-1 & SEFLG_SWIEPH` evaluated truthy and disguised failures as valid Swiss Ephemeris outputs. Fixed by checking `iflag < 0` prior to bitmasking and raising explicit `CalculationError`.
  2. **Hidden partial Moshier fallback:** Precedence previously reported `"swieph"` if *any* body came from `.se1` while others fell back to Moshier. Replaced with explicit `"swieph" | "moshier" | "mixed"` provenance aggregation (`EphemerisSource`).
  3. **Mid-chart lock gap & unlocked lazy initialization:** Converted `SWE_LOCK` from `threading.Lock()` to `threading.RLock()`, unified `timeconv.py` onto `get_swe_engine()` inside `SWE_LOCK`, and wrapped the full `compute_chart_facts()` sequence in a single `with SWE_LOCK:` guard so concurrent requests with different ayanamshas cannot interleave between Lagna and planetary calculations.
  4. **Ayanamsha error suppression & nutation parity:** Migrated `get_ayanamsha_degrees()` to `swe_get_ayanamsa_ex_ut` with explicit `SEFLG_SWIEPH | SEFLG_NONUT` (`0.00e+00` parity with `swe_get_ayanamsa_ut`) and failed closed on negative/non-finite results or `swe_houses_ex` `< 0` returns.
  5. **Longitude rounding boundary mismatch:** Quantized longitudes first modulo `360.0` before computing sign, degree in sign (`[0, 30)`), house, nakshatra, pada, and `f_lagna_sensitivity`, eliminating `29.9999996° -> 30.0` mismatches.
  6. **Exception discrimination & strict inputs:** Introduced `TimeConversionError(ValueError)` with `.code` distinct from unexpected `ValueError` or `CalculationError`; enforced finite numeric bounds on coordinates and `(-24, +24)` UTC offsets; restricted `ChartSettings.year_days` and validated preset consistency; bumped `ENGINE_VERSION` to `"0.2.0"`.
